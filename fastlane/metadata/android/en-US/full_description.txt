SMS Forwarder forwards the SMS you receive from the senders you choose to an HTTPS endpoint you configure.

<b>How it works</b>
- Every matching message is saved in a local queue and retried until the endpoint answers HTTP 200.
- A periodic sweep of the SMS inbox acts as a safety net, so messages are not lost if one is missed.
- Several destinations and rules, with custom method, headers and body.

<b>Features</b>
- Home screen with search and filters by delivery status.
- Option to hide the SMS text in the app.
- Soft delete: an SMS deleted from the app is not forwarded again even if it stays in the inbox.
- Pause forwarding at any time.
- Configurable sweep interval and history retention.
- Notifications per type of problem.
- Dynamic color (Material You).
- Spanish and English interface.

<b>Privacy</b>
- No Google services and no trackers.
- Messages go only to the destinations you configure.

<b>Permissions</b>
- RECEIVE_SMS: to be notified of incoming messages.
- READ_SMS: to sweep the inbox as a safety net.
- INTERNET: to send messages to your endpoint.
- Also used: notifications (alerts when something fails), start at boot (resume the queue) and ignoring battery optimizations (reliable background delivery).
